      Five rules that run every meeting
      
        
      
          
      A pre-read goes firstSent two business days ahead, one page, opening with their issue in their numbers.
      
          
      Three agenda itemsEach meeting has one job. If it needs a fourth item it needs a second meeting.
      
          
      Leave with a dateNo meeting ends without the next step, its owner and its date agreed in the room.
      
          
      Recap in one business dayIssue, solution, benefit, proof, next step. Under 300 words.
      
          
      Exit criteria gate the stageThe CRM stage only moves when the checklist is met. Three silent touches and the deal is paused with a follow-up date.
